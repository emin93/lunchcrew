## Full description
LunchCrew makes team lunch decisions fast and painless.

No more long chat threads or back-and-forth messages. Create a workspace, invite your coworkers with one link, and vote on today’s lunch spot in seconds.

### What you can do with LunchCrew
- Create or join instantly
- Run a daily lunch vote
- Vote in one tap
- Add new lunch ideas
- See results at a glance

### Built for real teams
LunchCrew is designed to remove friction from everyday lunch planning in offices, coworking spaces, and small teams.
